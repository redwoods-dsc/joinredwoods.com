_We're headed back to the UK!_

It started in a quick chat with Luke and Connie over at zeroheight about their plans for [Converge 2026](https://converge.zeroheight.com/). These days, there aren't a lot of in-person events focused on the design systems community, so we get excited when an organization steps up to make that happen. In 2025, as Redwoods was just finding its feet, we found ourselves gathering in Bristol, UK for zh Converge. Luckily for us, there is a beautiful stand of Coastal Redwood trees just outside the city. A few emails later, we had two sponsors ([zeroheight](https://bit.ly/3BSAOrk) and [Penpot](https://bit.ly/4aW1lDa0)) willing to buy us lunch and cover the rental of a HUGE bus to take us for a hike.

This all happened the day before the official Converge event and it was magical.

Hiking together gave us a chance to actually _know_ some folks before we all found ourselves sitting together at the venue. Between sessions, we found each other. At lunch, we hung out. All of this organic community goodness was a signal that what the design systems community needed more than anything was simply **time together**.

And that brings us to October of 2026, one year later. This time, Converge is happening in Newcastle, and we want you to come hang out with us!

### The plan

Since many Redwoods folks will be making the journey to England for Converge, we thought we'd make a couple stops along the way.

- London, October 19, in partnership with [Sparkbox](https://bit.ly/43njpAf) and [Dessn](https://bit.ly/4zptDQa)
- Manchester, October 20
- Newcastle, October 21 & 22, alongside Converge

Here's a bit more information about each stop.

#### Redwoods X London

![Redwoods X London, a design system gathering at Such and Such Bar, in London, England, on October 19 from 5:30–9pm](../../assets/_dessn-sparkbox-london-26.jpg)

On October 19, 2026 from 5:30–9pm, we'll be gathering the design system nerds in London for some appetizers, drinks, and collaborative problem solving. This event is happening in partnership with Dessn and Sparkbox.

More information will be posted here as it becomes available.

#### Redwoods X Manchester

On the afternoon and evening of October 20, 2026 we'll be gathering the design system nerds in Manchester for a hike and a pub crawl. Believe it or not, there are Redwoods in Manchester.

More information here as it becomes available.

#### Redwoods X Converge, 2026

On October 21, 2026, Redwoods will be hosting the opening night party for Converge. We've got a fantastic venue, a live version of The Question, and a slew of lighting talks from your peers. Zeroheight is buying the food and drinks, and we'll be going strong, into the night. More details here as they become available. For now, make sure to get your ticket to Converge (be sure to use the Redwoods discount).
