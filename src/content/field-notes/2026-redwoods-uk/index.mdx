  These days, there aren't a lot of in-person events focused on the design systems community, so we
  get excited when an organization steps up to make that happen.
</Note>
In 2025, as Redwoods was just finding its feet, we found ourselves gathering in Bristol, UK for zh
Converge. Luckily for us, there is a beautiful stand of Coastal Redwood trees just outside the city.
A few emails later, we had two sponsors ([zeroheight](https://bit.ly/3BSAOrk) and
[Penpot](https://bit.ly/4aW1lDa0)) willing to buy us lunch and cover the rental of a HUGE bus to
take us for a hike.

<Figure
  src={hikers}
  alt="A group of hikers pose, arms raised in celebration, in the woods outside of Bristol, UK on their way to see a small grove of Redwood trees."
  caption="Redwoods members, hiking to see the Redwood trees just outside of Bristol, UK!"
  border={false}
/>

This all happened the day before the official Converge event and it was magical.

Hiking together gave us a chance to actually _know_ some folks before we all found ourselves sitting together at the venue. Between sessions, we found each other. At lunch, we hung out. All of this organic community goodness was a signal that what the design systems community needed more than anything was simply **time together**.

And that brings us to October of 2026, one year later. This time, Converge is happening in Newcastle, and we want you to come hang out with us!

<Callout tone="dark">

## The plan

Since many Redwoods folks will be making the journey to England for Converge, we thought we'd make a couple stops along the way.

- London, October 19, 2026
- Manchester, October 20, 2026
- Newcastle, October 21 & 22 of 2026, alongside Converge

</Callout>

Here's a bit more information about each stop.

On October 19, 2026 from 6–9pm, we'll be gathering the design system nerds in London for some appetizers, drinks, and collaborative problem solving. This event is happening in partnership with [Dessn](https://bit.ly/4zptDQa) and [Sparkbox](https://bit.ly/43njpAf).

It's free to attend, but there is very limited space, so [request a ticket](https://bit.ly/rwdxlon-2026) soon.

### Redwoods X Manchester

<Figure
  src={redwoodsxmanchester}
  alt="Request a spot for Redwoods x Manchester, a design system gathering brought to you by Sparkbox"
  caption="Join us in Manchester on Oct 20 from 5pm."
  link="https://luma.com/dmuh5svu"
/>

On October 20, 2026, we're joining forces with [Sparkbox](https://bit.ly/43njpAf) to gather the design system nerds in Manchester for a pub crawl, a hike, and dinner. It's for anyone working on or with design systems who wants to spend some time in conversation with other system thinkers.

We'll meet at 5pm at Northern Monk in the Northern Quarter for a pint, then walk to Vimto Park to find the redwood trees. Believe it or not, there are Redwoods in Manchester. From there we'll land at Bundobust Brewery at 7pm for food and drinks, provided by Sparkbox.

There is limited space for this informal, fun event, so [request a spot](https://luma.com/dmuh5svu) quickly.
