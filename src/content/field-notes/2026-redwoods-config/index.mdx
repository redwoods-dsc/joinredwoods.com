### The backdrop

With the support of three sponsors: [Southleft](https://bit.ly/4desXmp), [Sparkbox](https://bit.ly/43njpAf), and the [AI & Design Systems Course](https://bit.ly/4wi5FEZ), we were able to rent two large buses and take over 50 design system nerds out to [Muir Woods](https://bit.ly/4eCyIwk). On our way back to town, we stopped to hang out and have some drinks. It was a fantastic start to Config.

It's been a few months now, and we've asked some of the members who joined us to share a bit about what the event was like for them.

### Stories

<Quote
  cite="Lauren LoPrete"
  role="Head of Design Foundations, Mercury"
  link="https://laurenloprete.design/"
>
  "Redwoods and friends spent a day hiking in Muir woods together before Config, and the whole thing
  was magical in a way that typical conference connections rarely are. There's something about being
  outside, among massive trees, that strips away the usual performance of networking. People get to
  be real with each other, and connect genuinely."
</Quote>

<Quote
  cite="Katie Orzek"
  role="Sr. Art Director, Digital Product, Fast Company + Inc."
  link="https://www.linkedin.com/in/katieorzeck/"
>
  "What first drew me into Redwoods was actually a chance to get into the woods and snag another
  stamp in my National Parks & Monuments Passport booklet. But I quickly gained so much more. I got
  to connect with DS people from publishing, discovering they've faced similar challenges I
  currently face myself. I am a designer who is responsible for building our baseline site system
  from the ground up and I'm the only team member well educated in this space. It's been lonely at
  times, and since I've become a member of Redwoods, it's been so nice connecting and talking with
  people who know exactly what I am experiencing."
</Quote>

<Quote
  cite="Emily Sullivan"
  role="Product Designer"
  link="https://www.linkedin.com/in/emily-a-sullivan/"
>
  "This year was my first time attending Config, and I was lucky enough to do so with the Redwoods
  group. I'm still early in my design systems journey, and I half expected to feel like an outsider
  among people who've been doing this for years. Instead, I was never once made to feel like a
  poser. Everyone made space for my questions and my perspective, no matter how green it was.
  Attending with Redwoods didn't just make the conference more fun. It made it more useful, because
  the best insights came from people, not presentations."
</Quote>

<Quote cite="Greg Johnson" role="Redwoods Member" link="https://www.linkedin.com/in/gregjdc/">
  "Even knowing Redwoods folks would be there, I wasn't prepared for how much that would change the
  experience. Every day I was able to find people to hang out with, people to sit with at the best
  talks like 'Design Systems Anarchy' by Redwoods' own Lauren LoPrete, and grab a bite or some
  drinks with fellow design systems nerds. Redwoods made all the difference. Not just in having a
  good conference, but in feeling like I belonged somewhere."
</Quote>

<Quote
  cite="Caleb Smith"
  role="Design System Lead, One North"

<Quote

</Quote>

<Quote
  cite="ToniAnn Drenckhahn"
